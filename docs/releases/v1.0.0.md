# Personal Finance OS 1.0.0

The first complete version: all eight build phases of the product specification. It is offline, private, and
available in Dutch and English.

## What is in it

- **Accounts and ledger**: current, joint, savings, credit card, pension, investments and cash accounts.
  - Quick Add (Ctrl+K), transfers, undo and reconciliation with a bank statement.
- **Import**: CSV and XLSX bank files with remembered column mappings, duplicate detection and payee rules.
- **Credit card and planning**:
  - statement periods and limits per card, never hard-coded;
  - recurring income and payments;
  - a forecast per account, a minimum buffer and safe-to-spend.
- **Budgets and analysis**: monthly budgets per category, compared with complete earlier months.
- **Assets and net worth**: valuations, ownership shares that change over time, net worth history and a wishlist
  with affordability dates.
- **Scenarios and the assistant**: what-if scenarios on top of the forecast, and a rule-based assistant that answers
  the common questions with figures and assumptions.
- **Reports (new)**: eight reports, shown on screen and saved as PDF or CSV.
  - Planned items appear apart from actual totals.
  - The PDF and the CSV always match the screen.
- **Backup and restore (new)**:
  - a verified one-file backup to a place you choose, with a reminder on the dashboard;
  - a guided restore that first keeps a checked copy of your current data.

## Installing and upgrading

- Run `PersonalFinanceOS-Setup-1.0.0.exe`. No administrator rights are needed.
- Installing over an earlier version keeps your data. The database is upgraded on first start, after an automatic,
  verified backup.
- Uninstalling never deletes `%LOCALAPPDATA%\PersonalFinanceOS`.
- The app is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm (**More info → Run anyway**).

## Privacy

There are no network connections, no telemetry and no cloud. The database is not encrypted, so protect your Windows
account with a password.
